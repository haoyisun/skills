# How to learn something new with Scholar

1. Install the `scholar` skill, together with `technical-diagrams` so it can draw diagrams.
2. Invoke `/scholar <topic, source, or learning goal>`.
3. Answer the questions about purpose, reading time, and what you already know. There are never more than five, and every question comes with a suggested answer you can accept.
4. Check the goal card. Correct anything that is wrong, then confirm it.
5. Scholar finds and vets sources first, then writes the material under `.scholar/study/`.

The depth tier decides the output:

- `quick 快读`: one overview, about ten minutes of reading.
- `guide 通学`: one complete study guide, one to three hours of reading.
- `mastery 精修`: a multi-file handbook for days or weeks, with a plan you can resume.

To continue later, run `/scholar 继续 <session folder>`, or just `/scholar 继续` to pick the most recently modified unfinished session.
